The problem and potential lift

Missed or deferred infusions leave treatment gaps and unused chair capacity. Nudge targets the practical and behavioral barriers that cause scheduled care not to happen.

Illustrative potential lift

Recovering 30% of lost infusions equals about 0.5 additional infusions per patient/year.

For 1,000 patients: about 500 returned visits/year, modeled at $0.5M–$3.5M in annual infusion value. Portfolio assumptions, not measured outcomes.

What Nudge does, and how

It converts missed-care risk into a clinically constrained workflow that can act on barriers and preserve human oversight.

  1. 1Personalize

    Uses adherence history and behavioral strategy to tailor outreach.

  2. 2Detect barriers

    Surfaces scheduling, transport, cost, education, and engagement friction.

  3. 3Resolve safely

    Reschedules within the ±3-day window and coordinates practical support.

  4. 4Escalate

    Hands clinical concerns to the care team with conversation context preserved.

How the prototype is built

Deterministic public demo for inspectable logic. Earlier full-stack build: React/TypeScript, Gemini function calling, FHIR-compatible data models, and Google Cloud Run.
